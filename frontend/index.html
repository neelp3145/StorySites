Helpful answers

A few things worth knowing.

Is a StorySite the same as a traditional website?

Not quite. A StorySite is created around your experiences, purpose, and personality. It can serve as a personal introduction, professional presence, creative showcase, or business presentation, with content designed around what you want to communicate.

Can I use my own photos and accomplishments?

Yes. Your own images, work, stories, and approved information help make the StorySite authentically yours.

Do I need to know anything about web development?

No. We'll help organize the content and handle the site's creation. You'll review the result before final delivery.

Can I host it myself?

Yes. You can receive your completed HTML deliverable for self-hosting, or ask us about managed hosting.

Will I be charged when I submit the form?

No. Submitting an inquiry or project request does not place a paid order. We confirm requirements and next steps before payment.

Do I have to sign a separate contract?

Most standard StorySites will use simple online Service Terms. We will first confirm your scope and price, then request explicit acceptance of your final order before payment or work begins. All standard orders are limited to branded informational StorySites.

1. What we're creating

We create a branded informational StorySite based on the agreed project description. Our service does not include ecommerce, booking systems, customer databases, or substantial integrations. Only the agreed sections, content and links are included.

2. Your materials and approvals

You provide accurate content, photos and approvals, and confirm you have permission to use submitted material. We may edit for clarity with your review.

3. Price and payment

Current starting prices are $249.99 for individual StorySites and $699.99 for business StorySites. Your final order will confirm the exact price and payment schedule before work begins. Sending an inquiry does not authorize payment.

4. Revisions and completion

Two reasonable rounds of revisions are included for the agreed scope if requested within 14 days of the first review. Requests outside the agreed scope may require a revised project description and additional creation fee; we do not offer custom application features. Timelines depend on receiving required materials and feedback.

5. Ownership and delivery

After full payment, you may use the delivered custom StorySite materials and code for your website, except for third-party licensed assets and reusable tools. You retain ownership of materials you supply. Domain names and third-party services are separately governed.

6. Hosting and renewals

Optional managed hosting is $99 per year for individual and business StorySites, when selected in the final order. Renewal pricing is disclosed before the next annual term. Hosting does not auto-renew unless you separately agree to recurring billing. Self-hosting is available through HTML delivery. Annual hosting covers deployment and routine hosting administration, but not ongoing content changes, redesigns, new features, domain registration, or third-party fees.

7. Cancellation and problems

If you cancel before work begins, prepaid creation fees are refundable. Once work begins, you pay for work completed up to the agreed project price; any unearned prepaid balance is refunded.
